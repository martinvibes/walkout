retention hazard cliff

The agent

Gemini reads each cliff window of the actual film — blind to the telemetry, so agreement between the picture and the numbers means something. Takes about a minute.

Method

Two witnesses that never spoke to each other.

01

Find the cliffs

Survival analysis over every playback heartbeat in ClickHouse. Hazard rate against a robust post-warmup baseline, a binomial z-test for significance, completions treated as censored rather than as exits.

02

Slice the audience

Each cliff is cut by device, platform, app build, CDN, region, locale and subtitle availability. Concentration — how much likelier one cohort was to leave here — is the discriminator.

03

Watch the film

Gemini reads that exact window of video and reports pacing, dialogue, what is on screen and any visible artefact. It is never told what the telemetry said.

04

Reconcile

Concentrated in one build with rebuffering above that cohort's own baseline is an engineering ticket. Flat across every cohort with clean playback is the cut. Inconclusive is said out loud.
